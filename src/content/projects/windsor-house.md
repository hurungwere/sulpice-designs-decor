Windsor House began as a dark, compartmentalised Edwardian and became a
serene backdrop for family life. We opened the plan around a sculptural kitchen
island, wrapped the walls in lime plaster, and layered tactile oak joinery to
soften the volume. Every finish was chosen to age gracefully.
